Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy describes how {{LEGAL_ENTITY}} ("zTTato", "we") handles information when you use zTTato Creator.

Information we process

When you authorize TikTok, we receive authorization tokens and basic account identifiers/scopes approved by you. When you upload content, we process the video file, filename, publishing settings, publishing identifiers and operational status needed to perform your request.

How we use information

We use information to provide TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API functions, maintain security, prevent duplicate publishing, diagnose failures and respond to support or legal requests. We do not ask for or store your TikTok password.

Sharing

We transfer content and requested metadata to TikTok only when you initiate and explicitly confirm the applicable action. We may use infrastructure providers acting on our instructions as processors. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Authorization and operational records are retained only as needed for the service, security, legal obligations and recovery. Uploaded local files should be removed under the operational retention policy. You may disconnect TikTok and request deletion through the application or contact us.

Security

Tokens are stored server-side and encrypted at rest. Production deployments require HTTPS, access controls, secret management, monitoring, backups and tested recovery procedures.

Your choices

You control whether to connect TikTok, which permissions to grant, whether to upload a draft or directly post, and the current publishing options TikTok makes available to your account.
