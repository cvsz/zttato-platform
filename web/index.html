Does zTTato post without my confirmation?

No. You review the selected content and settings, then confirm whether to upload a draft or request a direct post.

Does zTTato store my TikTok password?

No. You authorize the app on TikTok. The app stores authorization tokens on its server in encrypted form.

How do I disconnect or delete my data?

Use the account controls in the creator dashboard. The Privacy Policy explains deletion and retention.
